function and that type, not the route's internals: it never assembles a payload
itself. So holding the route's keys holds the page, and the page must not be edited.
`test/routes.bookings.test.ts:936-1002` already pins the happy path, the
`reschedulable` flip, the invoice amounts, the 403 for another customer and the 404
for a booking that does not exist.

the error envelope (`ApiHttpError`, `ok`/`created`, `handle()`) is untouched;
